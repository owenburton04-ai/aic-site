The short version

What the internship is.

The AIC internship digs into how AI is actually being put to work across business and finance. Over eight remote weeks you scope your own question in your corner of the business, publish a weekly brief and post, and finish with a capstone paper and deck. Strong members can be staffed on real, paid client work along the way.

Want the full picture?

The weekly rhythm, the research areas, the team structure, and what you walk away with are what we'll cover in your interview, and we're happy to talk it through before you apply. Just email owen@appliedintelligencecollective.com. One thing to know up front: the internship is unpaid.
