Our story

Why we started.

Applied Intelligence Collective began with a simple frustration: everyone in finance is talking about AI, and almost no one is saying anything real about it. So we decided to find out for ourselves.

Two best friends headed into finance, hooked on where investing and AI actually meet. We set out to understand it firsthand, then built AIC so other students could too.

Why we started

As students, we ran into two problems everyone entering finance faces: finding real-world experience outside the classroom, and understanding how AI is going to change the career ahead of us.

What finally made it click was hands-on work: an internship where our two interests came together and we learned by doing. The more we learned, the more we wanted to give other students the same shot. So we built Applied Intelligence Collective: a place with the structure, environment, and accountability to work out how AI is really being put to use across business and finance.

How we got here

From a research group to the spark.

We started out researching how AI is actually being used and publishing what we found in the open. Doing that work kept surfacing the same thing: plenty of companies know they should be using AI and have no idea where to start. So we put the research to work for them.

  1. 01

    We started researching

    Students digging into how AI is really used, sector by sector, and publishing it openly. That is still the engine underneath everything else.

  2. 02

    We kept finding the same gap

    Companies hear the hype, look at hundreds of tools, and either buy something they aren't ready for or stall out entirely. Almost nothing exists to get them unstuck.

  3. 03

    So we became the spark

    We make a company AI-aware and get it moving: the credible first step on its AI journey, not a transformation program.
